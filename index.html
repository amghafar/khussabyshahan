Since 2020

A family workshop, now online

Khussa by Shahan began as a small family workshop, hand-stitching khussas the way our grandparents taught us. Every pair still passes through the same artisan hands — cut, embroidered, and soled by people who take pride in getting the details right.

We built this shop so those same khussas could reach further than the local bazaar — without losing the craft that makes them worth wearing.
